# OCTOLOOP — 5-Minute Presentation Script (Team OCTOPUS)

**[0:00–0:30] Hook & problem**
> "A woman gets an abnormal mammogram. Then — silence. She is never called back. This happens to millions of patients every year, and it is not a clinical failure — it is a coordination failure. We are Team OCTOPUS, and this is OCTOLOOP: the Intelligent Screening Follow-up Platform that closes the screening-to-care loop."

**[0:30–1:15] The idea**
> "OCTOLOOP tracks every actionable screening result through a ten-step closed-loop journey — from result review, patient notification, confirmation, referral, appointment, consultation, treatment or surveillance, all the way to a documented closure. Every single case has a responsible person, a clear status, a due date, a full history, and automatic escalation when it goes overdue. No result is ever 'pending' forever."

**[1:15–2:30] Live demo — the loop in action**
> (Dashboard) "Here is our command center — open cases, overdue cases in red, follow-up completion rate, and screening-to-care conversion rate."
> (Register abnormal screening) "When we record an abnormal result, OCTOLOOP automatically opens a follow-up case and assigns a responsible clinician."
> (Case timeline) "This is the heart of the platform: the interactive patient journey. Each node is a real, timestamped, attributed event. We advance the case, send the patient notification, create and accept a referral, schedule and complete the appointment — the case moves forward automatically, and every step is audited."
> (AI panel) "Our AI assists operations — summarizing history, flagging overdue cases, highlighting missing steps, suggesting next actions. But it never diagnoses, never prescribes, and never closes a case. Clinical decisions stay with authorized humans."

**[2:30–3:30] Escalation, analytics, security**
> (Overdue + notifications) "When a case is overdue, configurable escalation rules fire automatically — alerting the responsible team and their supervisor."
> (Analytics) "And because every step is measured, the health system finally sees the full picture: time from screening to notification, time to consultation, lost-to-follow-up rates — all in one place."
> (Admin/audit) "Under the hood: role-based access control, bcrypt-hashed passwords, JWT auth, full audit trails, and input validation on every endpoint. Built on FastAPI, Next.js, and PostgreSQL — a real, production-grade stack, not a mockup."

**[3:30–4:30] Why we win**
> "Existing EMRs record care. They do not coordinate it. OCTOLOOP is purpose-built for the gap between screening and treatment — the gap where patients are lost. We turn a black box into a measurable, accountable, closed loop. For health systems, that means fewer missed cancers, fewer lawsuits, and healthier populations. For patients, it means someone is always watching their case."

**[4:30–5:00] Close**
> "Team OCTOPUS. OCTOLOOP — no result left behind. Thank you."
