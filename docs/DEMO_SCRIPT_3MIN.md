Use synthetic data. Log in as `coordinator.lee / Coord@12345` (demo only).

next step." **[TO FILL: one real, sourced statistic about results lost to follow-up]**

**0:50 – The journey (60 s).** Open the case timeline. Notify the patient, create the referral, schedule the
appointment, mark it attended; the case advances by itself. Show the AI panel: operational summary, missing steps,
disclaimer ("operational only; humans decide").

**1:50 – Nothing falls through (30 s).** Show an overdue case with an escalation event and the staff alert.

**2:20 – The patient's side (20 s).** Switch to Arabic (RTL) and open the patient portal
(`patient.ross / Patient@12345`): upcoming appointment, follow-ups, instructions.

**2:40 – Impact and next step (20 s).** Analytics: notification time, referral completion, lost-to-follow-up.
"Next: a 90-day pilot at one primary-care site." Only humans can close the case.
